Research programme

Individual differences, temporal dynamics, and interacting groups

My PhD work focused on personality-aware multimodal modelling of face-to-face emotion. Current work extends this trajectory toward small-group interaction, where affect, attention, participation, and physiology unfold across people rather than in isolation.

Selected research projects are conducted in collaboration with GN Advanced Science and investigate multimodal sensing and small-group interaction.

Scientific approach

Principles that shape the work

Complementarity

Signals have different meanings and time scales

Gaze, speech, facial behaviour, and physiology are not interchangeable. Models should respect their timing, uncertainty, and relation to the research question.

Generalisation

Evaluation follows people and groups

Careful splits, strong baselines, missing-signal tests, and cross-dataset analysis help distinguish genuine learning from participant or session leakage.

Human-centred practice

Interpretation stays close to evidence

Affective and cognitive models require conservative claims, transparent limitations, responsible data use, and attention to individual variation.
