GN Hearing · Advanced Science · CogniSense Lab

A conversation is more than sound.

Imagine a crowded café. Four friends, overlapping voices — and your attention shifts from one person to another. How could technology know who you want to listen to?

We explore that question using EEG, eye tracking, physiology, audio, video and AI. Join us to solve engineering challenges behind future intelligent hearing technology and human-centred wearables.

Student projects

A real engineering question. A measurable result.

You do not need to use hearing aids or know hearing science. We look for engineering curiosity, technical rigour and willingness to learn.

Build something real

Develop a sensing pipeline, prototype, evaluated model, reproducible benchmark or calibration method.

Scope it to your degree

BSc projects can focus on a defined component; MSc theses can tackle deeper modelling and evaluation.

Work across research and industry

Explore academic methods in GN Hearing Advanced Science, subject to project approval and appropriate supervision.

Important feasibility note

These are candidate thesis directions, not guaranteed funded openings. Device availability, recordings, data permissions, confidentiality, and academic supervision must be agreed before a project starts. Public datasets or simulated streams can provide independent starting points.
