## Excluded material

The website excludes:

- private GitHub repositories;
- internal GN systems, datasets, capture specifications, and operational details;
- procurement, budgets, staffing, and legal/administrative material;
- unreleased results or claims without a public source;
- personal data about research participants or colleagues beyond ordinary publication authorship.

A public protocol or paper does not imply that all underlying company data, tools, or infrastructure are public.
