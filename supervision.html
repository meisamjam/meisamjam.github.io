Hosting and data access

Projects may be hosted at ITU or organised as an ITU–GN collaboration. Company-linked topics use only agreed research descriptions and approved data access. Future-capture directions must include an existing-data fallback so that a student's progress does not depend on a recording milestone.

Typical BSc scope

A clear answer within a controlled scope

DataOne established dataset and one clearly defined target.
SignalsUsually one to three modalities with careful preprocessing.
MethodsTransparent baselines plus one meaningful extension or analysis.
ValueA defensible result even when a complex model does not outperform the baseline.

Typical MSc scope

A deeper methodological or scientific contribution

ContextTemporal, cross-person, cross-task, or cross-dataset modelling.
MethodsRepresentation learning, graph/transformer models, transfer, or uncertainty.
AnalysisRobust evaluation, ablations, failure cases, and theoretical interpretation.
ExtensionOngoing larger-scale recordings can extend—not define—the core thesis feasibility.
