About

A human-centred view of multimodal AI

I study how behavioural and physiological signals can help model affect, cognition, personality, attention, and social interaction. My work spans eye tracking, facial behaviour, speech and dialogue, EEG, EDA/GSR, PPG, wearable sensing, multimodal video, and interaction dynamics.

My research is connected to the ITU brAIn Lab at the IT University of Copenhagen, with selected projects conducted in collaboration with GN Advanced Science.

PhD thesis

Beyond Words: Personality-Aware Multimodal Modelling of Face-to-Face Emotion

Research interests

Understanding people through multiple signals

My research connects computational models with questions from cognition, emotion, social interaction, and human-centred system design.

Research trajectory

From individual emotion to interacting groups

My work develops a connected research programme: first understanding individual differences and felt versus perceived emotion, then modelling multimodal collaboration and group dynamics, and finally designing reproducible capture and learning methods that can transfer to realistic interaction.
